# Product or feature retirement

## Scope
Identify users, API consumers, service dependencies, data, credentials, domains, billing, and ongoing costs.

## Exit plan
Define notice drafts, dates, migration help, data export, retention and deletion, backup treatment, and support.

## Execution
List concrete disable, delete, revoke, archive, or cancel actions with their target, applicable authority, dependencies, owner, and recovery limit.

## Verification
Check user exit, exports, service consumers, access removal, deletion evidence, residual jobs, and final cost. Record actual receipts. A drafted shutdown plan is not a completed shutdown.

## Closure
Archive the useful decisions and evidence under the retention policy. Remove stale active instructions and close dependent work.
